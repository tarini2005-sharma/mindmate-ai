A few questions before your first check-in

These questions help MindMate personalize recommendations. They are not a diagnosis and can always be changed later. You can skip and answer later.

01

What's mainly bringing you here today?

Sets the starting emotion categories MindMate pays attention to.

02

How would you rate how you've felt over the past two weeks?

A one-time baseline, not a repeated diagnostic test.

Struggling a lot Doing well

03

Do you have someone you'd feel comfortable talking to if things got hard?

Shapes whether MindMate suggests reaching out to a person vs. a solo coping activity.

04

Are you currently seeing a therapist, counselor or doctor?

Lets MindMate position itself as a supplement, not a substitute, and adjust how it phrases suggestions.

05

What has helped you cope before?

Seeds your personalization scores so week one isn't a cold start.

06

Would you like to include optional GAD-7 / PHQ-9 screening questionnaires during future check-ins?

These are shown only as wellness context and never as a diagnosis.

07

Have you recently had thoughts of harming yourself?

This question is always asked privately so that MindMate can prioritize safety if needed.

If "Yes" is selected, MindMate will immediately display crisis resources before continuing. This information is used only to prioritize your safety.

08

Emergency Contact (Optional)

If you wish, you can provide someone we may suggest contacting during serious safety concerns.

Your answers remain private and are only used to personalize your own MindMate experience.
